Privacy at KICKZY

This page describes the main data-related features currently used by KICKZY, a browser-based football game. It should be read together with the privacy notices of any third-party services you choose to use.

Information stored in your browser

KICKZY may store game preferences and progress in your browser's local storage, including your chosen player name, coin balance, owned or equipped skins, match statistics, and sound settings. This information is stored by the browser on the device you use and may be lost if you clear site data. It does not automatically sync across different devices or browsers.

Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, KICKZY uses Firebase Authentication to support that sign-in flow. Google and Firebase may process information according to their own privacy terms. Do not sign in on a device you do not trust.

Online multiplayer

Online multiplayer uses PeerJS to help establish a connection between players. Network connection details needed to establish and maintain a connection may be processed by the service and by the players' browsers. Do not send private or sensitive information to another player through the game.

Advertising and cookies

Advertising may be added to eligible informational pages in the future. If Google AdSense ads are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalise advertising as permitted by applicable settings and law. The game interface itself is intended to remain free of ads during gameplay and navigation. This page must be updated if the actual advertising or tracking implementation changes.

Children and family supervision

KICKZY is a general-audience game. Parents or guardians should supervise account sign-in and advertising settings for younger players. Do not share passwords, contact details, or other private information in the game.

Changes to this page

This information may be updated when KICKZY's features or third-party services change. Please check this page again after major updates.
