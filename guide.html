How to play KICKZY

The aim is to score more goals than the opposing team before the match timer reaches zero. Move your player into a useful position, approach the ball from the direction you want to shoot, and use the kick action when you have a clear path toward goal. Watch the ball after a shot because rebounds can create another chance for either team.

1. Get into space

Try not to follow the ball directly behind your teammate. Moving into open space gives your team more passing and shooting options.

2. Aim your shot

Approach the ball with a clear angle toward the goal. A rushed shot from a poor position may give the other team possession.

3. Defend after attacking

If you move forward, keep an eye on the opposing player. A quick change of possession can leave your goal exposed.

Controls

Computer

Use the movement keys and kick key shown in the game interface. The exact control hints in the active game mode take priority if they differ.

Mobile

Use the on-screen joystick to move and the SHOOT button to kick. Keep your thumb inside the joystick area and make sure the game has enough screen space to show the controls.

Choose your match size

1v1 — One against one

A compact duel where timing and positioning matter. If you miss a challenge, recover quickly to protect the goal.

2v2 — Teamwork

Two players per team create more options. One player can pressure the ball while the other covers space near goal.

3v3 — More team play

With more players on the pitch, spreading out helps. Avoid crowding the ball and leave someone ready to defend when your team attacks.

Local two-player controls are not intended for two people sharing one phone. Use the available bot or online options on mobile.

Play online with a friend

Quick Play

Open the online menu and choose Quick Play to search for another player. Keep the page open while matchmaking tries to connect.

Room Code

One player creates a room and shares its code. The other player enters that code in the online menu and joins the same room. Both players should use the current version of the game.

Online play can depend on both players' connections, browser settings and whether the other player is still waiting in the room. If a connection fails, refresh both devices and create a new room.

Skins, coins and profile

The Store lets players preview supported cosmetic skins and equip unlocked items. Skins change the appearance of a player; they are intended as visual customisation rather than a gameplay advantage. Coins, equipped skins, player name and sound preferences may be saved in the browser on the device being used, so they may not automatically appear on another device or browser.

Only sign in on a device you trust. Never share your password or account recovery details with another player.

Tips for a better match

  • Approach the ball from the direction you want it to travel.
  • Look for open space rather than chasing the ball in a crowd.
  • After shooting, prepare for a rebound or a change of possession.
  • In 2v2 and 3v3, leave room for teammates to move.
  • If controls or the match look stuck, refresh the page and try again.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install KICKZY?

No traditional installation is required. Open the KICKZY website in a supported browser and start from the main menu.

Why can online play feel delayed?

Online movement depends on the network connection and the browser on both devices. A weak or unstable connection can make remote players appear delayed.

Why might my coins or skin differ on another device?

Some game progress and preferences are saved in that browser's local storage. Local browser data does not automatically synchronise between all devices.

Where do I start a match?

Return to the game using the button at the top or bottom of this page, then choose Play from the main menu.
